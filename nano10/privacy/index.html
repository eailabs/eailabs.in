1. Introduction

Welcome to Nano10 ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when preparing for competitive exams using our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data and usage information we collect, how it is processed and shared, and the controls you have over your data when you access or use the Nano10 mobile application (available on Google Play Store) and any associated web services (collectively, the "Service").

By downloading, installing, registering, or using Nano10, you agree to the collection and use of your information as outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with any terms herein, please do not access or use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our specialized topic-wise daily tests, synchronous live contest leaderboards, and All India Rankings (AIR), we collect several types of information from you.

Account Profile Data

When you sign in using Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication, we collect your unique Firebase UID, email address, display name, and profile picture URL.

Performance & Attempt History

We save your selected weekly topics, test attempts, submitted answers, completion times, and computed contest scores to compile the leaderboard rankings.

Device & Connection Info

We collect your IP address, operating system version, and device type. We also collect and store your **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token** to deliver alerts when a live test begins.

Diagnostic & Crash Logs

To ensure application stability and maintain connection sync during live events, we gather telemetry about application crashes, connection dropouts, and page load latencies.

3. How We Use Information

Your data is processed based on legitimate interests to provide, sustain, and elevate the educational features of the Service. Specifically, we utilize the data to:

  • ✓ Authenticate User Access: Secure login verification using Firebase Identity tokens and user state storage.
  • ✓ Conduct Live Sync Contests: Synchronize countdown timers and answer tracking for simultaneously active participants.
  • ✓ Formulate Rankings: Calculate test scores, compute performance speed, and publish All India Rank (AIR) leaderboards.
  • ✓ Push Notifications: Send timed push reminders via Firebase Cloud Messaging right before scheduled live test rooms open.
  • ✓ Prevent Abuse & Cheating: Prevent duplicate profiles and identify fraudulent attempts or unauthorized API calls during live rank runs.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data policies, we offer clear data retention standards and an active, straightforward pathway for complete account and information deletion.

Data Retention Limits

We retain your account data and historical exam attempts for as long as you maintain an active profile on Nano10. Device FCM tokens are updated dynamically and stale tokens are discarded after 60 days of inactivity.

Account Deletion & Data Removal

If you wish to terminate your profile and erase your information, you may do so through the methods below:

Email Request Email us directly at namaste@eailabs.in with your request. The deletion process will be initiated within 72 hours.
Interactive Deletion Builder Submit a deletion request using the automated email builder below to instantly pre-fill your request email.
Interactive Deletion Email Builder
What is deleted?

Personal attributes (names, profile picture assets, Google authentication ties), all past device tokens, and structural rankings will be purged. Aggregated scores will be anonymized for historical test scoring difficulty curves.

6. Children's Privacy

Nano10 targets high school students and young adults preparing for competitive engineering entrance examinations (such as JEE).

The Service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, distribute, or retain personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or legal guardian and you discover that your child under 13 has signed up or provided information, please contact us immediately. We will take rapid actions to remove such user records from our production servers.

7. Security Measures

We prioritize protecting your data and employ administrative, software, and hardware defenses:

  • All application communication to our backend APIs occurs exclusively over encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Authentication tokens (Firebase ID tokens) are verified on the backend on every API call.
  • Internal database systems are hosted behind virtual private clouds and network isolation walls.

Please remember that no transmission channel over the internet or storage database system is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have specific data rights, including:

To exercise any rights, simply contact us at namaste@eailabs.in. We will reply to all verified requests within 30 days.
